Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2025

Smart Sparks LLC ("Spark," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Spark dating app at smartsparks.app.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account InformationName, email address, date of birth, gender, sexual orientation (optional), profile photos
Profile ContentBio, interests, relationship goals, photos you upload
Location DataApproximate GPS location (used to show nearby users; stored as city/state only)
Usage DataSwipe activity, match interactions, messages, session logs, device/browser type
Payment DataBilling name, last 4 digits of card (Stripe processes full payment details — we never store card numbers)
CommunicationsMessages you send other users; support emails you send us

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your profile to potential matches based on your preferences
  • To facilitate connections (matches, messages, likes)
  • To process payments for Premium subscriptions via Stripe
  • To send push notifications about new matches and messages (with your consent)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and safety incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve and personalize the App experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Other users: Your profile (photos, name, bio, location city) is visible to other users based on your privacy settings.
  • Service providers: Firebase (Google) for auth/storage/database; Stripe for payment processing; Cloudflare for hosting. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Law enforcement: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users. See our Law Enforcement Protocols.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Location Data

We request device location to show you profiles nearby and to display your distance to other users. Your precise GPS coordinates are never stored or shared; we store only your city and state. You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time, though this will affect Nearby features.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your profile, photos, and personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records kept for 7 years for tax compliance).

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@smartsparks.app.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase Security Rules restricting data access, and Stripe's PCI-compliant payment processing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Spark is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will terminate it and delete their data immediately. Contact us at support@smartsparks.app if you believe a minor has registered.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, email support@smartsparks.app with subject line "CCPA Request."

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of Spark after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@smartsparks.app or visit our Contact page.
