How to Upgrade

Open the Spark App → tap your Profile tab → tap "Upgrade to Premium" → complete checkout securely via Stripe. Your Premium features activate immediately upon successful payment.

How to Cancel

Go to Profile → Settings → Manage Subscription, or email safety@smartsparks.app. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium access until the end of your current paid period.
