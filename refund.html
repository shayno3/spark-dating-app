Refund Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2025

This Refund Policy applies to all purchases made through Spark (smartsparks.app), operated by Smart Sparks LLC. Please read this policy carefully before subscribing to Spark Premium.

1. Spark Premium Subscription

Spark Premium is a recurring monthly subscription priced at $9.99 per month. Your subscription automatically renews each month on the date you originally subscribed, and your payment method on file will be charged unless you cancel before the renewal date.

How to cancel: Open the Spark App → Profile tab → Settings → Manage Subscription, or contact us at safety@smartsparks.app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you retain access to Premium features until that date.

2. General Refund Policy

All subscription fees are non-refundable except in the circumstances listed below. When you subscribe to Spark Premium, you gain immediate access to all Premium features, and by proceeding with the purchase you acknowledge that the digital services have been delivered in full upon subscription activation.

3. When Refunds Are Issued

We will issue a refund in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charge: You were charged more than once for the same subscription period due to a technical error.
  • Unauthorized charge: Your payment method was used without your authorization (you must also report this to your bank/card issuer).
  • Technical failure: A documented technical issue on our end prevented you from accessing Premium features for a significant portion of your billing period, and our support team was unable to resolve it.
  • Statutory rights: Where required by applicable consumer protection law in your jurisdiction.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email safety@smartsparks.app with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

  • The email address associated with your Spark account
  • The date of the charge
  • The reason for your refund request

We will respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and will be credited to your original payment method.

5. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank without first contacting us, we reserve the right to suspend or terminate your account pending resolution. Please contact us first at safety@smartsparks.app — we will do our best to resolve the issue quickly.

6. Free Trial Promotions

If Spark offers a free trial period in the future, charges will begin automatically at the end of the trial unless you cancel before the trial period ends. We will provide notice of upcoming charges before the trial converts to a paid subscription.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of Spark after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance.

8. Contact

For billing questions or refund requests: safety@smartsparks.app
