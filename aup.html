Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs how you may use the Spark platform ("App") operated by Smart Sparks LLC. By using the App, you agree to comply with this AUP in addition to our Terms & Conditions. Violations may result in immediate account suspension or termination.

1. Age Requirement

Spark is strictly for users aged 18 and older. You must not create an account or use the App if you are under 18. Any account found to belong to a minor will be permanently terminated. We take child safety extremely seriously.

2. Prohibited Content

You must not post, share, upload, or transmit content that:

  • Is sexually explicit, pornographic, or contains nudity unless mutually consented to through the App's designated features
  • Depicts or promotes self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders
  • Is racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise discriminatory
  • Portrays or glorifies violence, illegal weapons, or drug use
  • Infringes any third party's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights
  • Contains viruses, malware, or other harmful code
  • Is spam or unsolicited commercial advertising
  • Constitutes, facilitates, or promotes any illegal act or activity under applicable law

3. Strictly Prohibited Activities

The following activities are absolutely prohibited on Spark and will result in immediate permanent termination and referral to law enforcement:

  • Human trafficking or sex trafficking — You must not use Spark to recruit, harbor, transport, solicit, or obtain any person through force, fraud, or coercion for labor or commercial sex acts. Any content, communication, or conduct that facilitates human trafficking or sex trafficking is strictly prohibited.
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) — You must not upload, share, transmit, or possess any content that sexually exploits or depicts minors in any way. Spark has zero tolerance for CSAM and will immediately report any such content to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement agencies.
  • Impersonation — You must not impersonate any real person (living or deceased), entity, organization, or public figure in a manner intended to deceive, mislead, or defraud others. This includes creating fake profiles using another person's identity, photos, or personal information without their consent.
  • Illegal acts — You must not use Spark to plan, facilitate, promote, or engage in any activity that violates local, state, national, or international law, including but not limited to fraud, extortion, money laundering, drug trafficking, or any other criminal conduct.

4. Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

  • Harass, bully, threaten, stalk, or intimidate other users
  • Impersonate another person, entity, or public figure
  • Use fake or misleading profile photos or information
  • Solicit money, financial information, gift cards, or other valuables from other users
  • Attempt to move conversations off-platform to avoid safety monitoring before establishing trust
  • Use the App for commercial solicitation, escort services, or sex work
  • Create multiple or duplicate accounts
  • Share another user's personal information without their consent (doxxing)
  • Use automated bots, scripts, or scrapers to interact with the App
  • Attempt to circumvent geographic restrictions or access controls
  • Engage in any activity that could disable, overburden, or impair the App's infrastructure

5. Safety & Fraud

⚠️ Romance Scam Warning: Never send money to someone you met online. If a match asks for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or your financial information, report them immediately. Spark will never ask for payment outside our official checkout flow.

You must not use Spark to engage in romance fraud, financial scams, phishing, or any deceptive activity intended to harm or defraud other users.

6. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates this AUP, please report it immediately using the in-app "Report" feature available on any profile or conversation. You can also email us at safety@smartsparks.app.

7. Enforcement

Smart Sparks LLC reserves the right to investigate any reported violation and to take appropriate action, including removing content, issuing warnings, temporarily suspending, or permanently terminating accounts. We cooperate with law enforcement as outlined in our Law Enforcement Protocols.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy? Contact us at safety@smartsparks.app.
