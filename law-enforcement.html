Law Enforcement Protocols

Last updated: July 28, 2025

Smart Sparks LLC ("Spark") is committed to cooperating with law enforcement agencies in accordance with applicable law. This page outlines our procedures for responding to legal process, emergency requests, and official law enforcement inquiries regarding user data.

1. Submitting Legal Process

All legal process (subpoenas, court orders, search warrants, national security letters, or similar demands) must be submitted in writing to:

⚖️ Legal / Law Enforcement Contact

Company: Smart Sparks LLC
Email: safety@smartsparks.app
Subject line: LAW ENFORCEMENT REQUEST — [Case/Docket Number]

Requests must include: the name and contact information of the requesting officer/agency, a valid case or docket number, the specific data requested and the time period, the legal basis for the request (statutory authority, court order, etc.), and the signature of the supervising official where required.

2. Types of Requests We Can Fulfill

Depending on the legal basis provided, Spark may be able to provide:

  • Basic subscriber information (name, email address, registration date, last login)
  • Device information and IP address logs (where retained)
  • Account activity logs
  • Profile content and photos (where retained)
  • Message metadata (where legally required with appropriate court order)

We will provide only the minimum data necessary to comply with the legal request.

3. User Notification

In accordance with applicable law, Spark will attempt to notify affected users of legal process requests before disclosing their data, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so (e.g., by a gag order) or where notification would jeopardize an active investigation as stated in the legal request.

4. Emergency Requests

🚨 Emergency situations involving imminent risk of death or serious physical harm: We will voluntarily disclose information to law enforcement without legal process if we have a good faith belief that an emergency involving danger of death or serious physical injury requires immediate disclosure. Emergency requests must be submitted to safety@smartsparks.app with subject line EMERGENCY — IMMINENT HARM and must include the officer's name, agency, contact phone number, and a description of the emergency.

5. International Requests

Requests from law enforcement outside the United States must be submitted through a Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) or other legally recognized international mechanism. We do not accept direct foreign law enforcement requests outside of these channels except in genuine emergency situations.

6. What We Cannot Provide

  • Data that Spark does not possess or has not retained
  • Data without valid legal process (except in emergencies as described above)
  • Content of private messages without a valid court order or equivalent legal process

7. Data Preservation Requests

Law enforcement may submit written requests for Spark to preserve specific user data for up to 90 days (extendable) while formal legal process is being obtained. Preservation requests must identify the account(s) and the specific data to be preserved and must include a contact for the requesting agency.

8. Child Safety

Spark takes child protection extremely seriously. We report suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child exploitation to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A. Law enforcement agencies investigating cases involving potential harm to minors may contact us at safety@smartsparks.app with subject line CHILD SAFETY for expedited response.

9. Cost Reimbursement

Spark may seek reimbursement for costs incurred in responding to legal process as permitted by applicable law and pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 2706.

10. Contact

For legal process and law enforcement requests: safety@smartsparks.app with subject line "LAW ENFORCEMENT REQUEST."
